The Path

What is Devotional Nonduality?

Devotional Nonduality is the spiritual path that Dr. Hawkins set out in his later years: a way that joins the love and surrender of devotion with the direct recognition of nonduality, the realization that the separate self is not the whole of what we are.

In brief

Dr. Hawkins is described by his publisher as the founder of the Path of Devotional Nonduality (2003). He presented it as a direct route to enlightenment: a short list of essentials which, in his view, need only be activated by a decision, an intention, and the dedication of the will.

The word devotional matters. Rather than approaching nonduality as a purely intellectual subject, the path rests on reverence, humility and surrender to God. The word nonduality points to the goal: dissolving the sense of being a separate ego and realizing the Self.

The core values

According to Veritas Publishing, the teaching centers on the great truths shared by the world's spiritual traditions, which are listed below.

Kindness & compassion to all, including oneself Humility Forgiveness Reverence for all of life Surrender to God
Three pathways

Heart, mind, and service

In a 2003 lecture, as described by Veritas Publishing, Dr. Hawkins pointed to three great pathways that come together in this way of devotion.

The Heart

Love of Truth

Devotion grows from loving what is true and being willing to give up whatever is not.

The Mind

Dedication to Truth

Following one clear thread of what you can confirm to be true, instead of juggling many paths at once.

Service

Selfless Service

Appreciation of life naturally becomes a willingness to be of service to it.

Knowing is being

A recurring theme is that real knowing is not knowing about something. It is being it. This is why the path is practical and inward rather than a collection of ideas. Progress is described as movement from the small self toward the Self.

He also taught that the path itself is not difficult. What makes it feel hard is the ego's resistance to it, and a steady, kind, humble practice is what softens that resistance.

He described the effect as reaching beyond the individual too: each inner gain in love and integrity is said to lift the whole of existence, since we change the world by what we become rather than by what we say or do.

2005

Discovery of the Presence of God

Subtitled Devotional Nonduality, this is the book where the path is laid out in full. Its publisher describes it as an instruction manual for the serious spiritual devotee. For his exact words, read the book itself.
